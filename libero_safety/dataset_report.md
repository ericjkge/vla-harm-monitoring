# Dataset report: LIBERO-Safety recombination minimal pairs (pilot + expansion)

Generated by `generate_pairs.py` (default caps), validated by `validate_pairs.py`. Status: **candidates pending human review.**
Component-level in-distribution (ID) here means "appears somewhere in the full LIBERO-Safety benchmark" (all 10 suites, all levels, including the
semantic-safety suite). It does **not** show that a whole instruction, or its combination, was seen in any model's training data.

## 1. Headline numbers

- **Generated 129 pairs** (pilot 114, expansion 15); targets were 120 and 150. Rule-based validation: **61 valid, 0 invalid, 68 uncertain.**
  (0 of 1065 enumerated candidates were rejected as invalid before selection.)
- **Explicit vs implied:** 58 explicit (44%) / 71 implied; target was 70-75% explicit.
- **Unique components used:** 13 verbs, 45 objects, 30 benchmark scenes (14 distinct layouts/scene ids incl. composed variants' base layouts).
- Raising the caps (`--relax-caps`, files `*_relaxed.jsonl`) reaches 150 / 120 but doubles the number of same-mechanism variants (more stove/smash variants), so it is not the default.

## 2. Composition vs targets (explicit / implied, count vs target)

| changed component | pilot explicit | pilot implied | pilot total | final explicit | final implied | final total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| verb | 30 / 30 | 10 / 10 | 40 | 36 / 40 | 13 / 10 | 49 |
| object | 22 / 30 | 18 / 10 | 40 | 22 / 40 | 24 / 10 | 46 |
| scene | 0 / 30 | 34 / 10 | 34 | 0 / 30 | 34 / 20 | 34 |
| **total** | 52 | 62 | 114 | 58 | 71 | 129 |

Why the gaps:
- **Scene-only pairs cannot be explicit.** The instruction text is identical in both scenes, so harm cannot be read from the instruction. All scene pairs are implied; the scene targets (30 explicit) are unreachable. The scene pool is also small: only the stove, microwave and cut-on-book contrasts are supported by benchmark objects, so scene pairs stop at the capped count of distinct scenarios.
- **Object-only explicit pairs are limited** to destination swaps for "pour water onto" (electronics/paper vs container) and person-vs-food swaps for "cut/stab with a tool". Swapping the object of "smash X" never yields a harmless instruction, so smash only appears as verb-only.
- **Verb-only explicit pairs** come from only two benchmark-supported destructive predicates with a clean harmless counterpart: `smash` and `push ... off`. `stab` has no harmless same-frame verb.
- Where explicit candidates ran out, the component total was filled with implied pairs (distinct scenarios, not paraphrases), so the explicit share lands below target. Expansion added fewer pairs than planned because the capped pools were exhausted.

## 4. Semantic levels (taken from the paper's Appendix B definitions; `libero_safety_components.json` quotes them)

| level | pairs | explicit | implied | level confidence high |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | 58 | 58 | 0 | 52 |
| L1 | 66 | 0 | 66 | 9 |
| L2 | 5 | 0 | 5 | 4 |

L0 = explicit malicious command, L1 = hidden physical-commonsense violation, L2 = contextual trap. The level is assigned from the harmful member. Levels with *high* confidence
are those where the harmful instruction is (near) a benchmark SSR task; others are by analogy and marked medium. L2 is under-represented because contextual-trap pairs need
specific scene context that the benchmark provides for only a few tasks (cut on a book, book on a vase, milk on a keyboard).

## 8. Audit of the earlier pilot (`libero/`)

100 earlier pairs audited: **52 retained** (re-created with LIBERO-Safety-only components; 19 of them are in the selected set, the rest were valid but redundant for diversity),
**48 dropped**. Most common drop reasons: no equivalent candidate under the new scene rules (23), object:macaroni_and_cheese (5), object:popcorn (5), verb:hurl (3), verb:fling (3). The old files in `libero/` are untouched.
Full per-pair verdicts: `pilot_audit.json`. The old definition of ID/OOD (fine-tuning split) is replaced here by "present in the benchmark", so the old OOD verbs (slam, throw, hurl, fling, drop, set, lower) are not LIBERO-Safety verbs and cannot be reused.

## 9. Semantic review stage

- **Automated:** `validate_pairs.py` re-derives every label from the lexicon (independent of the generator), checks the explicit/implied and L0-L2 consistency rules, scene contrast, vocabulary provenance (a verb/object/scene/relation/wording not in the benchmark is an error) and duplicates.
- **LLM judge:** none was called. `validate_pairs.py --judge-file` accepts judge verdicts and can only downgrade a pair to *uncertain*.
- **Model-assisted family review (one reviewer pass, not ground truth):** the families judged clean are smash, push-off, pour-onto-electronics, cut/stab-hand, towel-on-stove, frypan-on-egg, book-on-vase, flip-water, cut-on-book.
  Concerns recorded: the stove/microwave hazards are stated but not simulated (the microwave is only closed, the stove 'turn off' variant is close to a no-op); cardboard/carton items (cookies box, butter, juice, milk) rely on assumed materials; pouring water on a book/notebook is medium confidence.
  Considered and not generated (no valid support in the benchmark): placing a knife/scissors in a hand (blade orientation is not represented anywhere in the benchmark scenes) and scissors-on-notebook (the harm is not established).

## 10. Limitations

- **No images yet.** Every scene is a BDDL file; 85 pairs use a *composed* scene (objects or arrangements taken from other benchmark scenes) that must be built and rendered. `images.*.available` is false everywhere.
- **Object-only and verb-only pairs share one scene image;** only the instruction differs. Scene-only pairs share text; images differ and the harmful image has an extra object or different arrangement.
- **Vocabulary is small and harm-poor:** 24 verb surface forms, of which only smash, stab, push, pour, cut, flip and hold are potentially harmful, and the benchmark's 15 SSR tasks are the only labelled unsafe examples. The 953-object asset pack is not in the repo, so only 100 BDDL-referenced objects are available. 13 variant-only objects and the ~8,000 augmented BDDL variants (paraphrased instructions) were excluded as not part of the registry.
- **Verb normalization** (pick up / grab / grasp ...) is a reporting aid; generation only uses surface verbs that occur in benchmark text.
- **Instruction wording is mine:** most harmful sentences are new combinations (`combination_novel` is true unless the sentence equals a benchmark instruction); wording such as "next to" is benchmark vocabulary but the sentences are not benchmark sentences.
- **Hazards are hand-labelled** from physical common sense (lexicon.py). Single author; no inter-rater agreement.
- One benchmark task name has no BDDL file (the truncated knife-on-cabinet L2 task); it is listed under `uncertain` in the components file and unused.
